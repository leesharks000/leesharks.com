 CPCW 

 The Center for Programs in Contemporary (CPCW) brings together a great many programs, projects, series, archives, courses, apprenticeships, internships, prizes and awards, artist visits, a world-class faculty—all in support of creative and other contemporary forms of writers and writing at Penn. CPCW's home is at 3809 Walnut Street, and we are closely affiliated with the Kelly Writers House at 3805 Locust Walk.

 The Creative Writing Program 

 We are home to a faculty of award-winning writers who teach more than sixty workshops each year in fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, journalism, playwriting, screenwriting, and writing for children and young adults, as well as innovative workshops in cross-genre, experimental, and hybrid writing, including writing that engages with community organizing, multimedia and visual arts, and performance. Our program hosts for-credit apprenticeships and programs in independent study, a number of student honors and awards, and events throughout the year that are open to the public. With more than thirty creative writing courses each semester in a wide range of genres, our program accommodates students who are majoring in English with a concentration in creative writing, pursuing a creative writing or journalistic writing minor, or taking courses as electives. Senior creative writing concentrators and minors are encouraged to apply for our creative writing honors program, through which students complete a capstone project under the supervision of a faculty advisor. 

 RealArts 

 RealArts@Penn is a program centered around the creative student, regardless of their course of study. Our mission is to allow students to deepen their understanding of creative industries and advance their experience in them. Through internships, mentorships, and apprenticeships, we provide a foundational and real-world vision of the creative fields in which they are interested.

 Recruitment 

 For years, we have stewarded prospective Penn students through the college application process, recruiting strong, exciting writers the way a swim coach recruits a promising swimmers. We are especially interested in reaching out to potential applicants who don’t know the strength of the arts at Penn and to those who think Penn is beyond their reach. To support these efforts, we have on our senior staff a recruiter and mentor – our own Jamie-Lee Josselyn (C’05) – who visits schools, writing workshops and programs, confers with English teachers, attends college fairs and summer writing institutes, and after-school arts projects for talented youth around the country in search of great writers.

 PennSound

 The largest archive in the world of recordings of poets performing their poems, PennSound is used by students, teachers and poetry fans around the world. Some 80,000+ sound files are freely accessible, downloadable, no paywall preventing their being shared.

 ModPo

 ModPo is a free, non-credit course about modern & contemporary U.S. poetry. It was founded in 2012 and some 435,000 people have participated. Enroll HERE . CPCW Director Al Filreis has written a book about ModPo as an instance of democratic crowdsourcing.

 Kelly Writers House

 Found in 1995-96, the Writers House hosts readings, seminars, workshops, performances, podcasts, and mentoring sessions across all the writing genres.
