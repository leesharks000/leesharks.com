 What does it mean to write with AI?

 The Wordcraft Writers Workshop is a collaboration between Google's
 PAIR and
 Magenta teams, and 13 professional
writers from a diverse set of creative writing backgrounds. Together we explore
the limits of co-writing with LaMDA 
and foster an honest and earnest conversation about the rapidly changing
relationship between technology and creativity.
