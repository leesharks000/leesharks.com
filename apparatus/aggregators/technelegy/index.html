What its record can settle

  • how the artist states the persona Technelegy was made (her own poems, research, family memory, recordings) and since when
  • which roles the artist assigns the persona in FOUR CORE TEXTS

What it cannot settle

  • which base model or provider underlies the customized model, or the full training data
  • which lines of any work were generated and which written
  • whether the artist's priority claims ('an early paradigm', 'prescient artifact') hold historically

Kept apart

  • NOT the book Technelegy (2021): the book of the same name is a related object made jointly by Stiles and the persona
  • NOT the customized models themselves: the pages describe them without naming base model, provider or full training data
  • NOT an independent civil person: the artist presents it as an AI poet she conceived and as her alter ego
  • NOT independently established priority: 'helped establish an early paradigm' and 'prescient artifact of the pre-ChatGPT era' are the artist's own words
  • NOT BINA48, for which Stiles serves as Poetry Mentor
