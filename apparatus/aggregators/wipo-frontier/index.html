Founded2019 (the WIPO Conversation)

What its record can settle

  • which AI and IP questions WIPO put to its multi-stakeholder forum, session by session, and when
  • WIPO's stated frame for the IP system and frontier technologies (human-centered innovation and creativity)

What it cannot settle

  • what any Member State's law holds on AI authorship (the forum is discussion)
  • what was said in any session (session records linked, unread)
  • anything about pseudonymous or heteronymous authorship

Kept apart

  • NOT a treaty or a norm-setting committee: the Conversation is a discussion forum
  • NOT limited to AI: its frontier technologies include quantum computing, the metaverse, blockchain and data
  • NOT continuing as the AI forum: IP and AI discussion moves to the Global Forum on IP and AI from November 2026
  • NOT a registry of works or authors

Relations

  • neighbour: Copyright and Artificial Intelligence (the U.S. Copyright Office's AI page lists 'WIPO on Artificial Intelligence' among its Related Resources)
