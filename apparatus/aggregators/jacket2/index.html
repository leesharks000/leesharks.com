Jacket2

https://jacket2.org/

peripheral comparatorAvant-garde and experimental archivesactive
Jacket2 is an online magazine of commentary on modern and contemporary poetry and poetics, hosted by the Kelly Writers House at the University of Pennsylvania and published by Al Filreis; it keeps the complete archive of Jacket, the magazine John Tranter founded in 1997. Its unit is the piece, published as it is ready under the names of its author or authors.

What it is

Jacket2 publishes articles, reviews, interviews, discussions, archival documents and podcasts, as it is ready, in its own phrase "Not unlike a daily news forum", and keeps an index of everything it has published. Whether it publishes poetry is stated two ways: Wikipedia, "As of 2023", says it "does not publish poetry", while its own index lists a Poems section and its home page carries "a new poem by Michele Leggott".

From its /non article, sentences 1, 2, 3, 4

Who makes it

The Kelly Writers House, at 3805 Locust Walk, Philadelphia, counts Jacket2 among the publications it sponsors or hosts. Filreis, the Writers House's founder and faculty director, is the publisher. Wikipedia names Jessica Lowenthal, director of the Writers House, as associate publisher, and Julia Bloch and Michael S. Hennessey as editors. The home page names an editorial assistant, Emi Wong Shing, and signs a memorial to Alice Notley "Jacket2 Editors"; its About page returned "The requested page could not be found."

From its /non article, sentences 6, 7, 8, 9, 10

Jacket and the succession

Tranter founded Jacket in 1997 as an internet quarterly and published and edited it until 2010, when he gave it to the University of Pennsylvania. Jacket2 maintains the complete archive of Jacket, forty issues from 1997 to 2010. The year Jacket2 began is given differently by its sources: its home page celebrates a fifteenth birthday on April 5th with no year, and Wikipedia, to which the title redirects, describes it as "(2010-)".

From its /non article, sentences 12, 13, 15, 16

Reissues and podcasts

It reissues seven small-press magazines and presses, each with its editors, from Roof (1976–79, ed. James Sherry) to Aufgabe (2001–2014, ed. E. Tracy Grinnell et al.). Its podcasts include PennSound podcasts, Sounding Translation and PennSound Rewind. PoemTalk, "a collaboration of the Kelly Writers House and PennSound", is produced and hosted by Filreis and numbered to #222 on the home page.

From its /non article, sentences 18, 19, 22, 23

Place in the field

Each piece stands under the names of those who made it: co-authors, interviewers and translators are named in the byline, as in "Uldis Bērziņš, translated by Kevin M. F. Platt and Sintija Ozoliņa", and one piece is signed collectively. Its media highlight credits the sculpture "God" as "Made collaboratively" by Morton Schamberg and Elsa von Freytag-Loringhoven. No policy on pseudonyms, heteronyms or collective names is stated.
