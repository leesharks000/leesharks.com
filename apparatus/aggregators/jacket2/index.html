What it cannot settle

  • Jacket2's editorial policy and editors (the About page returned 404 when saved)
  • how it handles a contributor writing under a pseudonym or heteronym (not stated)

Kept apart

  • NOT Jacket magazine (1997 to 2010), which it keeps as an archive
  • NOT a recordings archive: its podcasts come with PennSound, which holds the recordings
  • NOT a peer-reviewed journal by any statement in the saved sources: it publishes 'as it is ready', like a daily news forum
  • NOT an author-page archive: its unit is the published piece
