Chat GPT Is Eating the World is a news site and set of trackers on lawsuits against AI companies over copyright and torts, published at chatgptiseatingtheworld.com by an operator its pages do not name, with monthly archives from January 2023. Its unit is a news post on a case or filing, usually with the complaint or order to download.

What it is

It tracks the latest developments in AI, by its own account, and its tagline reads "AI copyright & tort litigation, tracked in real time." The site and its newsletter state that it is not affiliated in any way with OpenAI or with the developers of the other AI systems it discusses. Its Substack calls itself "My personal Substack" and reports over 2,000 subscribers. It names a sister site, Claude Is Eating the World.

Its trackers cover AI copyright cases, AI authorship, AI tort lawsuits, AI data center lawsuits, BIPA litigation, and web scraping and AI agents; its menus add a tracker of lawsuits against OpenAI and pages on single cases, Thomson Reuters v. ROSS Intelligence among them. Beside them stand a map of copyright suits against AI companies, a master list of lawsuits last updated on 5 December 2025 and a master chart of claims last updated on 31 March 2026.

What it counts

Its counts differ by tracker and by date. The latest map story gives 157 copyright suits against AI companies after nine were added; the U.S. map gives 148 suits in the U.S. as of 8 October 2026; the world map gives 130, 100 in the U.S. and 30 elsewhere, as of 5 April 2026, with 143 to stand when it is updated. Its own positions

Its author takes positions in the litigation it tracks, seeking leave to file an amicus brief in the OpenAI multidistrict litigation and writing opinion pieces. It finds from its own trackers that plaintiffs' firms are driving the AI copyright litigation, and frames the suits as the "AI Copyright Wars".
