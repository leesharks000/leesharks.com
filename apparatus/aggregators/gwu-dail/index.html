The archive's reading. The archive reads DAIL as a secondary legal strand: a docket-level record of disputes over AI from complaint forward, whose bearing on how authorship is made and attributed runs through whatever authorship cases it holds; the saved page shows none, and the practices the disputes concern lie outside its record.

What its record can settle

  • whether a known lawsuit involving AI is entered, and its course from complaint forward, including cases without published decisions (subject to its search, unread here)

What it cannot settle

  • how a court ruled on AI authorship in any named case (no case was read here)
  • the number of cases held or the jurisdictions covered (not on the saved page)
  • anything about pseudonymous or heteronymous authorship

Kept apart

  • NOT limited to copyright or authorship: its scope runs from hiring, credit and sentencing algorithms to generative AI training and AI companion liability
  • NOT a database of published decisions only: it covers cases from complaint forward; opinions sit in a companion database
  • NOT complete by its own statement: it is still under development
