DAIL – The Database of AI Litigation

https://blogs.gwu.edu/law-eti/ai-litigation-database/

peripheral comparatorBoundary and governanceactive
DAIL, the Database of AI Litigation, is a database of ongoing and completed litigation involving artificial intelligence and related topics, kept by the Ethical Tech Initiative on George Washington University's site. It enters a case from the complaint forward, whether or not the case generates a published decision, and holds issued opinions in a companion database.

What it is

Its intended scope runs from algorithms used in hiring, credit and criminal sentencing decisions to generative AI training and AI companion liability. By its own statement it is still under development and will continue to expand; its page gives no date of founding or of last update. The Ethical Tech Initiative describes itself as an interdisciplinary collaboration where law and ethics meet computer science and engineering, media and public affairs, and social entrepreneurship. The page names no school and no staff and states no licence. A second address, etidail.com, serves a page under the database's name whose text is built by a script.

What it keeps

The page shows Scope, Guide, Credits and Suggestions tabs and search filters, and it takes suggestions of cases from readers. It states no count of cases, no jurisdictions and no start date. It names no parties, authors or works, so whose authorship it keeps and under what name are not recorded. It can show whether a known lawsuit involving AI is entered and how the case has run, including cases without a published decision.
