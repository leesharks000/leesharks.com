Pessoa Digital

direct caseHeteronymy, persona and pseudonymous authorshipactive
The archive's reading. The edition keeps the place where Pessoa's authorship was planned: the lists and plans in which he conceived his work, set beside what he published. That is a record of the names' works as Pessoa assigned them before publication fixed them, which the census reads beside #940's account of distinct named authorial functions. How it marks the names themselves stays unread until its own pages can be fetched.

Who holds ita digital edition hosted by the DCH, edited by three named scholars, funded by the FCT; licence not stated in the saved source
Authorial function and responsibilitynot recorded as a rule; the edition's object is the planning in which Pessoa conceived his work, and its index of persons and network of persons are where the saved account places the figures
