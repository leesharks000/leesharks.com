Pessoa Digital (pessoadigital.pt) is the digital scholarly edition "Fernando Pessoa. Projects and Publications": it edits the handwritten notes, lists and plans with which Pessoa conceived his work, set beside the works he published in his lifetime. Begun in 2014 by the Universidade Nova de Lisboa and the Cologne Center for eHumanities, it is edited by Ulrike Henny-Krahmer, Pedro Sepúlveda and Jorge Uribe and hosted by the DCH.

What it is

Its object is Pessoa's work between planning and publication, traced through the notes, lists and plans set beside what he published. The edition contains text transcriptions in TEI, facsimiles, indexes of persons, journals and titles, and visualisations of its data as timelines and a network of persons, built on an eXist database with XQuery and XSLT scripts.

Makers and history

The Lisbon founding partner is named two ways: the Laboratório de Estudos Literários Avançados (ELAB) on the CCeH's project page, and the Institute of Literature and Tradition (IELT) in the University of Rostock's announcement of 2023. The edition is supported by Portugal's Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia, and for 2023 it received funding from NFDI Text+ to integrate it into the research data infrastructure of Text+.

From its /non article, sentences 5, 6, 7

Its framework project, Estranhar Pessoa, has run since mid-2013. The edition was at the CCeH from 2014 to 2016 and has been ongoing since; its web presentation was first launched in 2017, and the cooperation was extended to EAFIT University in Medellín, from 2019 by Rostock's account. The two accounts differ on Pessoa's birth year: 1883 at Rostock, 1888 on the CCeH's page.
