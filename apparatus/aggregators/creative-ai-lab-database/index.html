The archive's reading. The database records tools for making with machine learning, and the saved launch reports show no record of works or of how their authorship is attributed; it meets the census question only at its edge, where the Lab's programme asks about 'creative agency, authorship and ownership' and where the launch credits a named curator for a contribution. The row rests on launch reports from HOLO, King's College London and Serpentine: the database itself is unread.

What its record can settle

  • that the Lab launched a database of creative AI tools and resources on 9 July 2020, and who runs and funds it
  • that its launch list of 65 tools was contributed by a named curator
  • what the Lab's research questions are (AI/ML tools and artistic practice, curatorial change, critical practice, aesthetics)

What it cannot settle

  • which tools or resources the database now holds (unread)
  • whose works were made with those tools, or under what name
  • how authorship of AI-made art is attributed in the Lab's commissions
  • whether the database is still maintained: the saved sources say 'Ongoing' and show no entry dates

Kept apart

  • NOT read from the database itself: the Notion page is script-rendered and unread; the row rests on launch reports
  • NOT a record of works or authors: it aggregates tools and resources
  • NOT the Creative AI Lab as a whole: the Lab's events, tutorials and position paper are its wider programme
  • NOT independent of the Lab: King's College London and Serpentine are the Lab's partners; HOLO is the third party
