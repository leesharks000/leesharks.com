What its record can settle

  • how a group of 19 professional writers understands authenticity when co-writing with AI, in their own accounts
  • how those writers respond to personalized against non-personalized AI writing support
  • whether 30 surveyed readers could tell AI-assisted work from the writers' solo-written work

What it cannot settle

  • how authorship of the co-written texts was credited or named
  • how writers or readers outside these samples understand co-writing
  • which AI tools, models and tasks were used (in the full paper, unsaved)
  • how a sustained named corpus or persona develops with AI over time

Kept apart

  • NOT a census of writers: 19 professional writers interviewed
  • NOT a record of credit or naming of co-written work
  • NOT a test of detection at scale: 30 readers surveyed
  • NOT a study of personas or heteronyms
