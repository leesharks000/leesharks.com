The archive's reading. C2PA authenticates a chain of custody for assertions: a validated manifest establishes that signed assertions are associated with the asset, well formed and untampered, and attributes them to a signer and a claim generator; it is no determination of authorship, and the specification refuses value judgments on the assertions. Against #940 its responsibility layer is the signer's credential, and a human's own account of what was made is gathered and left unattributed, so the standard carries provenance of process and has no field for the authorial function or a chosen name.

What its record can settle

  • whether a manifest's assertions are bound to an asset, correctly formed and untampered since signing (Well-Formed, Valid)
  • whether the signing credential chains to a trust anchor the validator accepts (Trusted)
  • which actions a manifest records, with which software agents, and whether a c2pa.created action declares generative AI as the digital source type
  • to whom a Valid manifest's claim can be attributed: the claim generator

What it cannot settle

  • who authored a work: validation establishes association, form and freedom from tampering, and attributes the claim to a claim generator and the assertions to a signer
  • whether a signed assertion is true (left to the consumer)
  • whether a human statement of authorship is correct: such entries are gathered and are not attributed to the signer
  • anything about an asset that carries no manifest
  • how an authorial name, pseudonym or heteronym is held: the standard metadata assertion supports no Dublin Core creator field

Kept apart

  • NOT a determination of authorship: validating signed assertions establishes association, form and freedom from tampering, and makes no value judgment on them (the selection review's point, borne out by §1.2 and §14)
  • NOT a detector of AI content: it records what a claim generator declares, such as a digitalSourceType
  • NOT a record of authorial names: the standard metadata assertion omits a Dublin Core creator field, the schema.org assertion is deprecated and the actors field was removed in 2.0
  • NOT a statement of who or what can be an author: it is a technical standard for provenance data
  • NOT applicable to assets without a manifest: adoption is opt-in
