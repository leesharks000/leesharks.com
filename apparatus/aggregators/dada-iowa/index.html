What its record can settle

  • whether the Archive holds or catalogues a named Dada work or scholarship on it
  • whether a scanned original of a Dada periodical issue or book is in the Digital Dada Library
  • under which names the Archive files a dadaist who published under more than one

What it cannot settle

  • full-text searches of the scanned documents (page images only)
  • complete runs of most periodicals
  • which of an author's names the Archive treats as the author's own (it lists both, in varying order, with no stated rule)

Kept apart

  • NOT a full-text corpus: the scans are page images only
  • NOT complete for most periodicals by its own statement
  • NOT an interlibrary lending service: it has no staff for loan requests
  • NOT a reprint edition: it scans originals and omits copyrighted matter from the few reprints it includes
  • NOT a surrealism archive, though it publishes Dada/Surrealism: its collection focuses on the historic Dada movement
