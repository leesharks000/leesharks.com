Arquivo Pessoa

http://arquivopessoa.net/

direct caseHeteronymy, persona and pseudonymous authorshipactive
Arquivo Pessoa (arquivopessoa.net) is the online edition of Fernando Pessoa's published work, the Obra Édita, edited in 2008 by Obra Aberta CRL under the conception and direction of Leonor Areal, and an update of the CD-ROM "MultiPessoa — Labirinto Multimedia" of 1997. Its texts stand under the names Pessoa wrote as: its author index lists 39 names, the orthonym among them, with joint attributions as entries of their own.

What it is

Its home page presents it as "Obra de Fernando Pessoa". It is one of the two active parts of the MultiPessoa portal: the database, "base de dados da maior parte da obra pessoana, com capacidades de pesquisa de texto complexas", beside the Labirinto, an introduction to the work with readings of 120 poems.

From its /non article, sentences 1, 2

How it holds the names

The author index is headed "Ortónimo, pseudónimos e heterónimos de Fernando Pessoa" and lists 39 names alphabetically,. The orthonym is one entry among them, and each name leads to its own index of texts. Joint attributions are entries of their own, among them "Wardour + Pessoa" and "Álvaro de Campos e Ricardo Reis". The heading names three classes and the entries carry none, so the index does not say which names are heteronyms and which pseudonyms, and it has no class of semi-heteronym.

From its /non article, sentences 4, 5, 6, 8

The work is indexed in twenty-two sections: four by name, the orthonym's poetry among them, then the Livro do Desassossego and Mensagem, then sixteen by kind, among them "Poesia Inglesa". Four of the names are also voices: Ricardo Reis, Alberto Caeiro, Álvaro de Campos and Fernando Pessoa each have a reader of their own.

From its /non article, sentences 13, 16

Makers and holder

The technical sheet credits the edition by role: conception and direction to Leonor Areal, the edition to Obra Aberta CRL in 2008, and production to Areal and Elisabete Vilar. It lists the Instituto de Estudos sobre o Modernismo of the Universidade Nova de Lisboa and Assírio&Alvim under "Parcerias". A blog post of 2018 for the Lisboa Pessoa Hotel credits the making to the Instituto itself, "Between 1997 and 2008".

From its /non article, sentences 17, 21, 22

What it states of its limits

It offers researchers the work "(quase completa)". The digital edition complements the printed ones, with automatic search as its main advantage, and though it holds a large part of Pessoa's known work it "não apresenta quaisquer notas de edição, como variantes e outras explicações indispensáveis".

From its /non article, sentences 28, 29

A third phase was planned to add "os facsimile dos originais do espólio existente na Biblioteca Nacional", and on the home page FACSIMILE stands unlinked beside OBRA ÉDITA and INFO. The edition holds the published work, distinct from Pessoa's estate and papers.

From its /non article, sentences 25, 26, 32

Place in the field

The archive reads it as a differentiated authorial corpus held in an edition: the works stay under the names they bear, the orthonym is one name among 39, while the whole stays answerable to one name, "Obra de Fernando Pessoa". That is the arrangement #940 states as its counter-principle: "The authorial function is attributable through the civil bearer without being reducible to that bearer."

From its /non article, sentences 41, 42

Asked "Arquivo Pessoa" on 1 November 2026, Google's AI Overview composed the edition from three cards. The Capture Registry found that its list of categories drops the orthonym's poetry and carries neither the pseudonyms nor the joint attributions, giving the heteronyms as contents of a corpus.

What its record can settle

  • under which of Pessoa's names a published text is held
  • which names Pessoa wrote under, as the edition indexes them, joint attributions included
  • who made the edition and from which print edition

What it cannot settle

  • which names the edition counts as heteronyms and which as pseudonyms (the saved index lists them together)
  • Pessoa's unpublished papers (the site is the published work, Obra Édita)
  • the textual basis of a given text beyond its bibliographic source (the transcription norms were not read, and the edition gives no notes of variants)

Kept apart

  • NOT Pessoa's estate or papers: it edits the published work (Obra Édita); the estate's facsimiles are a planned third phase
  • NOT a critical edition: it gives no edition notes or variants
  • NOT a reduction of the names to Fernando Pessoa: the index keeps 39 names, joint ones among them
  • NOT a classification of each name: the saved index does not mark pseudonym or heteronym
  • NOT pessoadigital.pt, a separate resource in this census
