How authorship is made and named: a meta-aggregator / Electronic Poetry Center

Electronic Poetry Center

https://writing.upenn.edu/epc/

infrastructureAvant-garde and experimental archivesactive
The archive's reading. The archive reads the EPC as infrastructure that holds a field's authors beside the field's own discussion (the Poetics List, the Buffalo Poetics Program), so authorship stays with a community's record of itself. The saved sources show author pages under poets' names and no account of chosen names or of how a contribution inside a work is attributed.

What its record can settle

  • whether the EPC holds an author page or a digitized text for a named poet or journal
  • what the Buffalo Poetics Program and the Poetics List produced, in their archives

What it cannot settle

  • the rights status of a digitized text (not stated)
  • how a poet writing under several names is filed (no naming policy in the saved sources)
  • a stable address for older resources (links from epc.buffalo.edu may break)

Kept apart

  • NOT a recordings archive: its sister site PennSound holds the audio
  • NOT a rights holder by any statement in the saved sources
  • NOT limited to digital poetry: it also holds contemporary formally innovative print poetries and poetics
  • NOT one founding date: its About page gives 1995, Wikipedia gives July 10, 1994

Relations

  • neighbour: PennSound (the About page calls PennSound its sister site at CPCW, Penn; home-page memorial entries link to PennSound pages)
  • neighbour: UbuWeb (Wikipedia (third party) states the EPC is partnered with UbuWeb)
  • neighbour: Jacket2 (the home page's memorial entries link obituaries and tributes at Jacket2)
