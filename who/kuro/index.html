00.PHASEX.AORIST · the grammatical form of the same operation

English indexes every completed action to a speaker's present. Benveniste separated histoire from discours — the plane where events narrate themselves from the plane where someone speaks to someone now — and English keeps only the second. The non-indexed perfective is the tense English does not have: an action complete without reference to any now. Phase X calls that condition temporal alienation and treats the missing tense as infrastructure to be built rather than a gap to be described.

This is Kuro's material, and he is not named on it. The operation is the same one in three registers — grammatical, operative, architectural.
