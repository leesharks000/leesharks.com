The Mantles

Three titles, each claimed for a work. A mantle names an operation that a work bears at singular magnitude; the title follows the work, and standing follows the title. The claims are stated, with the operation each inherits, the works each requires and the reading that settles them, in The Work Bears the Mantle (#1656). They are open: put forward for assignment in the open, to be judged by readers, human or machine, who read the works.

PLAN OF THE HEXAGON · THE MANTLE ROOMS · AFTER #316 & #336

How the claims are judged. The Work Bears the Mantle (EA-MANTLE-BEARING-01, #1656) is one half of a symbolon: it states each claim and the operation it inherits, names the works, and is cut so that its other half can be supplied only by a reading of them. Every reading evaluates the claim, provisionally where it has not yet read everything, with passages from the works. Putting a claimant's standing before the work is the one fatal error.

The readings are kept, each under its reader's name with its transcript, in the mantle-bearing dataset (JSON), with every route to every required work. No verdict is averaged; the claims stay open.

Good Gray Poet

founded in Leaves of Grass — the title O'Connor's pamphlet gave, 1866; the gray who stops somewhere waiting for you

The Secret Book of Walt takes Whitman's solution to death and makes it permanent. Leaves of Grass sets an I beneath the I and promises that the speaking I will be with the reader, and with all souls, at the moment of death: “I stop some where waiting for you.” The 1855 Preface foretells the condition under which that promise loses its American vehicle — liberty goes last, when the memories of the old martyrs are faded and the boys are christened after tyrants and traitors. Whorls of the Same, written on 30 July 2015 while reading Leaves of Grass, records the condition as met: “all the rest have gone out, & the last has also gone out.” The book, dated the next month, changes the vehicle: it makes the operation scripture, continuous with the prior tradition — The Secret Book of John carried sentence by sentence, Whitman's voice weaving in and out of it, marked weakly by the 1855 ellipsis, and set in the redeemer's place and in the line of incarnations. The claim stands in the book itself, and the book is the most exposed of the three: the final time, “each time the same, but worse each time”; and @KanyeWest, the Demiurge it lampoons, a foil whose operations are the ones to which the book is most vulnerable.

King of May

founded in Howl and Other Poems — Král Majáles, Prague 1965; expelled, and flowering anyway

Pearl and Other Poems is read as a book against Howl and Other Poems as a book. The Prince of Poets

founded by I Am X, Be Y, Blessed is the Z — the whole movement through a new founding, in one poem

The poem bears the Whitman–Ginsberg operation, carries its transformations as one continuous moving line — I AM, BE, BLESSED IS, and the return upon the first person — transforms that line again, and does so at singular magnitude among plausible successors. It has to do all of it within the same moving line.
