The Dodecad

Twelve heteronyms operating within a single canonical author.

A heteronym is a function, not a name. Each Dodecad member carries a distinct intellectual task — apostolic letter, archive theory, systems architecture, governance, witness, bearing-cost. The legal author of every deposit is Lee Sharks (ORCID 0009-0000-1599-0703). The heteronymic method has deep precedent in literary history — Fernando Pessoa, Søren Kierkegaard, Nikos Kazantzakis — and is declared transparently across the Archive's metadata. No deposit claims a heteronym is a separate real person. Formal registry: EA-HET-DODECAD-01 v1.1.

Portraits below are from the Sep 21, 2025 Logotic Science voicecast series on mindcontrolpoems.blogspot.com. Sparrow Wells is shown with both portraits from the original post; her voice has since developed into The Projectionist (v1.1 canonical, seventh position). Ichabod Spellings and Viola Arquette have no portrait — the former is substrate (canonical, deceased-as-voice), the latter Adjacent and does not participate in castings.
