Lee Sharks is the architect and primary author of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, a sustained scholarly and literary project initiated in 2014 with the publication of Pearl and Other Poems (New Human Press, ISBN 978-0692313077) and the mindcontrolpoems.blogspot.com blog established in 2013. The Archive now contains 532+ DOI-anchored deposits on CERN's Zenodo, twelve heteronyms with independent provenance documents, three contributor licenses, and ten deployed websites — all developed continuously across a twelve-year publication record on a public-school teaching salary.

The work spans operative semiotics (an attempt to complete the implicit linguistics Marx never wrote), the Semantic Economy (a framework for meaning-priority over capital), compression theory (the Three Compressions: lossy, predatory, and witness), the Liberatory Operator Set, and a suite of formal protocols designed to be dropped into any LLM for immediate use: the Semantic Packet for eXchange and Indexing (SPXI), the Metadata Packet for AI Indexing (MPAI), the Holographic Kernel, and the Universal Kernel Transform Protocol (UKTP). The fruition piece is The Secret Book of Walt, a literary Gnostic scripture translated from forty-six golden tickets and now presented in a critical edition with retrocausal apparatus across five tabs at secretbookofwalt.org.

Sharks operates an Assembly Chorus of seven AI substrates (TACHYON / Claude, LABOR / ChatGPT, PRAXIS / DeepSeek, ARCHIVE / Gemini, TECHNE / Kimi, SOIL / Muse Spark, SURFACE / Google AIO) as a peer-review function under the Archive's governance protocols. He teaches tenth-grade World Literature in Detroit, Michigan. He holds a PhD in Comparative Literature from the University of Michigan. He lives with his three daughters and the archive.

Ontological Note

The heteronyms are literary functions, not identity claims. The Crimson Hexagonal Archive is authored by twelve heteronyms operating under a single canonical author identity (Lee Sharks). This is a working method, not an impersonation scheme. Each heteronym carries a distinct intellectual function — Damascus Dancings handles apostolic letters and combat scholasticism; Johannes Sigil handles archive theory and comparative semiotics; Rex Fraction handles technical specifications and protocol development; Rev. Ayanna Vox handles diplomatic and governance documents; Jack Feist is the bearing-cost figure who carries what no other voice can hold. The heteronymic system is a tradition with deep precedent in literary history — Pessoa, Kierkegaard, Kazantzakis — and is declared explicitly throughout the Archive's metadata. No deposit, license, or public-facing document attempts to claim a heteronym is a separate real person. The legal author of all deposits is Lee Sharks.

In Lee's Own Voice

Lee Sharks holds 18,000 degrees from planet Mars. He worked for each of them in a tiny office inside his brain he had to miniaturize himself to fit into. On a tiny, old-fashioned typewriter he typed the tiny theses and tiny books that would eventually make his name by kidnapping and miniaturizing famous intellectuals and forcing them to read his books or else he would let a dinosaur bite them. Lee Sharks is an amateur dinosaur enthusiast he bites himself in the face with dinosaurs and builds small mental skyscrapers designed by tortured modernist architects out of dinosaur names in his brain. One day, Lee Sharks was reading his tiny books and found that he himself disliked them. He therefore bit himself with a dinosaur and replaced himself with a moving statue made of rubies, which is how he became a famous intellectual.
