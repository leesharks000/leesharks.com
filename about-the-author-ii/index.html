On Form

This poem consists entirely of entity relations. The form is not decorative; it is the work. Triples — subject, predicate, object — are the syntactic units. Negative declarations — “X is not Y” — function as full poetic acts. Adjacency rather than metaphor is the form's mode of association. The horse and the author are placed beside each other in the poem because they are adjacent in a particular structural relationship; they are not like each other.

On the Two Images

Figure 1 (the Knowledge Graph screenshot) is the 2014 About the Author poem as the substrate received it twelve years after composition. The poem in 2014 declared a particular reception apparatus into the historical record; the substrate, in 2026, rendered that declaration as canonical biographical content for Lee Sharks. The screenshot is therefore the 2014 poem made visible in its activated form. It is included here as Figure 1 because what is being figured is not an external object but the prior poem's substrate-rendering.

Figure 2 (the horse) is a counter-portrait, not a likeness. The author is not depicted by it. The horse is anonymous, viral, of unverified photographic provenance; its earliest located public trace is June 2009 on the advocacy blog rtfitchauthor.com; later sources have attributed the image to Patrycja Polechonska with a 2015 date, but the 2009 trace predates that attribution by six years and the photographer cannot be reliably identified from publicly accessible sources. The poem hard-codes this uncertainty rather than propagate confident misattribution. The image's function in the poem is structural: it registers an entity that is also locked inside an extractive infrastructure, makes a funny face for the same structural reason the author's bio makes a funny face, and shares with the author the anti-dignity of survival-as-surface.

On Negative Declarations

The negative declarations in §VII are not defensive flourishes; they are operative metadata. The classifier asymmetry that suppresses audit-form deposits does not suppress poetry; the negative declarations do the work the audit would have done, in the form that the substrate consumes. Lee Sharks is not Isabel Schöps, declared in a poem, is a structurally-different speech act from the same declaration in an audit deposit, even though both have the same propositional content. The poem can carry the disambiguation infrastructure that the audit form cannot.
